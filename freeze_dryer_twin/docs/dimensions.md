## 2. Condenser Collector Chamber
- **Chamber Material**: Seamless 316 stainless steel deep-drawn cylinder with electropolished interior.
- **Inside Diameter**: $\varnothing 254.0\text{ mm}$ ($10.0"$).
- **Chamber Depth**: $280.0\text{ mm}$ ($11.0"$).
- **Flange Outer Diameter**: $\varnothing 292.0\text{ mm}$ with silicone perimeter sealing ring ($7.0\text{ mm}$ cross-section).
- **Internal Cooling Coil**: 316 stainless steel seamless tube $\varnothing 12.7\text{ mm}$ ($1/2"$ OD), 5-turn helical coil lining the chamber wall, pitch $45.0\text{ mm}$.
- **Drain Port**: Located at low-point sump of collector chamber, $\varnothing 9.5\text{ mm}$ stainless barb tube routing to front fascia silicone drain line with pinch clamp.

## 3. Clear Acrylic Drying Drum Manifold
- **Cylinder Dimensions**: Outer diameter $\varnothing 305.0\text{ mm}$, wall thickness $6.35\text{ mm}$, height $381.0\text{ mm}$.
- **Top Lid**: Removable $\varnothing 320.0\text{ mm} \times 12.7\text{ mm}$ cast acrylic plate with central silicone lifting knob ($\varnothing 38.0\text{ mm} \times 25.0\text{ mm}$).
- **Port Layout**: 12 Quick-Seal valves arranged in two staggered vertical tiers of 6 ports each:
  - **Tier 1 (Lower)**: Elevation $Y = 589.0\text{ mm}$ ($+120\text{ mm}$ from manifold base); angular positions $\theta = 0^\circ, 60^\circ, 120^\circ, 180^\circ, 240^\circ, 300^\circ$.
  - **Tier 2 (Upper)**: Elevation $Y = 729.0\text{ mm}$ ($+260\text{ mm}$ from manifold base); angular positions $\theta = 30^\circ, 90^\circ, 150^\circ, 210^\circ, 270^\circ, 330^\circ$.
- **Quick-Seal Valve Body**: Neoprene/delrin valve body $\varnothing 38.0\text{ mm} \times 55.0\text{ mm}$ outward radial projection.
- **Operating Lever**: 3-position lever ($45.0\text{ mm}$ length):
  - `CLOSED`: Vertical $90^\circ$
  - `VACUUM`: Horizontal left $0^\circ$
  - `VENT`: Horizontal right $180^\circ$
