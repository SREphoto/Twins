Engineering Schematics & System Architecture

How Samples Get Tested (Optical vs. Fluidic Routing)

Liquid samples do NOT route or pump through tubing inside a UV-Vis spectrophotometer! The liquid sample is pipetted into a precision 10 mm quartz cuvette and placed inside the light-tight sample compartment. The monochromatic LIGHT BEAM is what travels through the machine, penetrating through the stationary liquid in the cuvette. The detector measures transmitted photons ($I$) versus incident photons ($I_0$), calculating Absorbance via the Beer-Lambert Law: A = -log₁₀(I / I₀) = ε · c · l

Electrical Architecture & Physical Wiring Continuity

Dual-power safety isolation: AC mains routes through double-pole switch & ceramic fuses into universal SMPS. Dedicated high-voltage ignition circuitry drives the D₂ arc lamp, while precision constant current drives the Halogen bulb. Low-noise detector analog signals route via shielded RG-174 coaxial cables directly to a 24-bit delta-sigma ADC.

Cooling Fan Fastener Breakdown & Rear Bulkhead Legend

In accordance with Rule 1 (Exhaustive Procedural Detail), the cooling fan is secured by exactly six (6) genuine DIN 912 M4 hex socket cap screws. Four screws clamp the steel wire finger-guard through silicone vibration dampers to the fan frame and cowl bracket. Two screws bolt the bracket foot directly to the die-cast baseplate chassis.
