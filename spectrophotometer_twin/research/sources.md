# Research Sources — UV-Vis Spectrophotometer

Primary literature, manuals, and instrument catalogs referenced for `spectrophotometer_twin`.

---

## 1. Instrument Service & User Manuals

1. **Shimadzu UV-1900i Spectrophotometer User Manual**
   - Czerny-Turner monochromator grating optics (1200 lines/mm LO-RAY-LIGH grating).
   - Wavelength range: 190.0 to 1100.0 nm; accuracy $\pm 0.1$ nm at $656.1$ nm ($D_2$).
   - Automatic lamp switching at 340 nm between Deuterium and Tungsten-Halogen.
   - 6-cell automatic carousel turret mechanics and microswitch lid sensor.

2. **Agilent Cary 3500 Multicell UV-Vis Spectrophotometer Specification Sheet**
   - High-throughput multicell Peltier-controlled sample chamber architecture.
   - Beer-Lambert linearity metrics up to 4.0 AU.

3. **Thermo Scientific Genesys 150/180 Technical Guide**
   - Industrial benchtop tactile membrane keypad layout and 7-inch color display graphics.
   - Standard baseline correction and calibration algorithms.

## 2. Chemical Reagents & Reference Absorption Spectra

1. **Potassium Permanganate ($KMnO_4$) in Deionized Water**
   - Characteristic vibronic absorption spectrum in the green region ($500–550$ nm), $\lambda_{max} = 525$ nm.
2. **Double-Stranded Calf Thymus DNA in TE Buffer**
   - Characteristic nucleic acid absorption band at $\lambda_{max} = 260$ nm, standard purity ratio $A_{260}/A_{280} \approx 1.85$.
3. **Coomassie Brilliant Blue G-250 / BSA (Bradford Assay)**
   - Absorption shift from cationic red form (470 nm) to stable unprotonated blue complex at $\lambda_{max} = 595$ nm.
4. **Methylene Blue**
   - Organic dye strong electronic transitions at $\lambda_{max} = 664$ nm.
