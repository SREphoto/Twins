3D NAVIGATION ARCHITECTURE

Navigation Controls & Walking Tool

📱 Mobile Walking Tool (Zero Pinch-to-Zoom)

  • Right Side (~27% of Screen) — Walking Speed Zone: Touch and drag your finger UP to move forward & accelerate; drag DOWN to slow down, stop, and back up. Lift your finger to stop smoothly.
  • Left & Center (~73% of Screen) — Aim Direction: Drag with your thumb or finger anywhere outside the right edge zone to smoothly aim yaw & pitch. Both touch zones work simultaneously with zero UI boxes on screen.
  • No Pinch-to-Zoom Movement: Pinch zooming is disabled on mobile so multi-touch speed + aim never conflict.
  • Tap Any Machine or Room Pill: Tap any room pill along the bottom dock or tap any 3D instrument to glide directly into eye-level or operator view; drag UP/DOWN on the right side anytime to walk away.

🖥️ Desktop Controls

  • Walk Mode: W A S D or Arrow keys (hold Shift to sprint), click viewport to lock mouse look, ESC to reset.
  • Interactive Architecture: Click any door to open/close, click vertical walnut blinds, or tap the 3D wall control panels (blinds, lights, 4K projector, conference phone & privacy frosting).
  • Overview / Orbit Mode: Left-drag to orbit, right-drag to pan, scroll wheel to zoom, click any instrument to glide in.
  • Sidebar: Press Tab or tap the arrow tab to collapse/expand the Lab Director panel.
