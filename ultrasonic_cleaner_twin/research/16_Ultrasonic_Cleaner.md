# Ultrasonic Cleaner

## Operation Principle

An ultrasonic cleaner uses high-frequency sound waves (20–40 kHz) to create cavitation bubbles in a liquid cleaning solution. Piezoelectric transducers (PZT crystals) bonded to the stainless steel tank convert high-frequency electrical signals into mechanical vibrations. During the rarefaction phase, microscopic vacuum bubbles form and grow; during compression, they implode, releasing localized shockwaves (~5000°C, ~1000 atm) that blast contaminants off surfaces. A heater (100–500 W) warms the solution to 40–60°C for enhanced cleaning.

## Key Functions

1. **Lab glassware cleaning** — removing residues from beakers, flasks, pipettes
2. **Instrument component cleaning** — HPLC injectors, filter frits
3. **PCR consumable cleaning** — removing DNA/RNA contamination
4. **Sample preparation** — degassing mobile phases, extraction
5. **Degassing** — removing dissolved gases from liquids

## Physical Features on the 3D Model

### Cleaning Tank

- **Tank Material** — stainless steel 304/316, deep-drawn single piece
- **Capacity** — 0.5 L to 50 L (common bench: 1.9, 2.8, 5.7, 9.5, 18, 28 L)
- **Tank Walls** — 0.8–1.5 mm, electropolished
- **Fill Lines** — "MAX" and "MIN" markings
- **Drain** — slight slope to drain

### Lid

- **Hinged Lid** — stainless steel or clear polycarbonate
- **Gasket** — silicone or vinyl, reduces evaporation/noise
- **Handle** — inset or pull
- **Observation Window** — clear acrylic (on some)
- **Vent Holes** — for pressure equalization

### Transducers

- **Piezoelectric Transducers (PZT)** — 4–12 per tank
- **Frequency** — 37 kHz (standard), 25 kHz (heavy), 45–130 kHz (delicate)
- **Bonded** — to tank bottom or sides

### Heating System

- **Heating Elements** — sheathed resistive, 100–500 W
- **Temperature Range** — 30–80°C
- **Thermostat** — adjustable or fixed

### Degas / Sweep / Pulse Functions

- **Degas** — continuous ultrasound, no frequency sweep (5–15 min)
- **Sweep** — shifts frequency ±2 kHz to prevent standing waves
- **Pulse** — variable on/off intervals for difficult cleaning

### Drain (larger models)

- **Drain Valve** — ball valve with hose barb
- **Location** — lowest point of tank

### Housing

- **Case** — painted steel or stainless steel
- **Feet** — 4 rubber anti-vibration
- **Ventilation** — grilles for electronics cooling
