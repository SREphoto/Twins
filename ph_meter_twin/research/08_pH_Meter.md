# pH Meter

## Operation Principle

A pH meter measures hydrogen ion activity using a combination glass electrode. The pH-sensitive glass membrane at the tip generates a voltage proportional to H⁺ concentration, measured against a reference electrode (Ag/AgCl in KCl). The Nernst equation gives ~59.16 mV per pH unit at 25°C. A high-impedance amplifier (>10¹² Ω) converts the mV signal to pH (0.00–14.00). Automatic temperature compensation (ATC) corrects for temperature effects. Calibration with standard buffers (pH 4.00, 7.00, 10.00) adjusts slope and offset.

## Key Functions

1. **pH measurement** — 0.00–14.00, resolution 0.01 or 0.001
2. **Temperature measurement** — via ATC probe, −5 to 105°C
3. **Calibration** — 1 to 5 point with automatic buffer recognition
4. **mV/ORP measurement** — oxidation-reduction potential
5. **Ion concentration** — with ISE electrodes (selected models)
6. **Data logging** — store readings with date/time
7. **Alarm functions** — pH out-of-range, calibration due, electrode check

## Physical Features on the 3D Model

### Electrode Assembly

- **pH Electrode** — combination glass, ~12 mm × 120–200 mm
  - **Glass Bulb** — pH-sensitive membrane, spherical or conical
  - **Reference Junction** — ceramic frit or open junction
  - **Body** — glass or epoxy, with filling port (refillable models)
  - **Storage Cap** — with storage solution (3M KCl)
  - **Cable** — BNC or DIN, 1–3 m
- **ATC Probe** — thermistor in stainless steel sheath, 3–5 mm × 100–150 mm
- **Cable Connector** — 2.5 mm phone jack or mini-DIN

### Meter Body

- **Case** — molded plastic or metal, splash-proof (IP54/IP67)
- **Display** — LCD or TFT, angled for viewing
- **Membrane Keypad / Touchscreen**

### Electrode Stand / Arm

- **Articulated Arm** — metal/plastic, 300–500 mm, height adjustable
- **Electrode Holder** — spring-loaded clamp
- **Magnetic Stirrer Platform** — optional, built-in

### Connectors (Rear/Side)

- **BNC Connector** — pH electrode, labeled "INPUT"
- **ATC / Temp Connector** — 2.5 mm jack or mini-DIN, labeled "ATC"
- **GND Terminal** — banana jack (for shielding)
- **Stirrer Connector** — 8-pin mini-DIN
- **Power Input** — 12 VDC barrel jack
- **USB-A, USB-B, RS-232, Ethernet**
