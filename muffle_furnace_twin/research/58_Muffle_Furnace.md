# Muffle Furnace

## Operation Principle

A muffle furnace is a high-temperature heating device used for ashing, heat treatment, and material processing. The
heating elements (MoSi₂, Kanthal, or silicon carbide) are located outside a ceramic muffle (chamber), isolating the
sample from combustion byproducts. The chamber is heated by radiation and convection. A digital PID controller maintains
precise temperature, and a thermocouple (Type K, R, or S) provides feedback. Maximum temperatures range from 1100°C to
1800°C depending on the heating elements.

## Key Functions

1. **Ashing** — determine inorganic content of organic materials
2. **Heat treatment** — annealing, sintering, tempering
3. **Gravimetric analysis** — loss on ignition
4. **Fusion** — preparing samples for XRF/ICP analysis

### Heating Elements

- **MoSi₂ (Molybdenum Disilicide)**: 1800°C max, for high-temperature muffle furnaces
- **Kanthal A-1 (FeCrAl alloy)**: 1200–1400°C, for standard furnaces
- **Silicon Carbide (SiC)**: 1500–1600°C
- **Ceramic Fiber**: Wound heating element molded into ceramic fiber insulation

### Temperature Control

- **Controller**: Digital PID with auto-tune
- **Display**: Dual LED or LCD — actual temp + setpoint
- **Thermocouple**: Type K (1100°C), Type R (1600°C), Type S (1800°C)
- **Ramp/Soak**: Programmable (up to 8–16 segments)
- **Safety**: Door interlock (heater off when door opens), over-temperature limit

### Insulation

- **Material**: Ceramic fiber (alumina-silica) — low thermal mass
- **Thickness**: ~4–8 inches (102–203 mm)
- **Efficiency**: Cool to touch exterior even at 1100°C interior

### Refractory Hearth Plate

- **Material**: Alumina (Al₂O₃) or recrystallized silicon carbide
- **Shape**: Flat plate at chamber bottom
- **Size**: Matches floor dimensions

### Power Requirements

- **1100°C Models**: 120V/15A (1–3L), 208–240V/20–30A (9–20L)
- **1200°C Models**: 208–240V/20–40A
- **Connector**: NEMA 5-20 (120V) or NEMA 6-20/30 (208/240V)
