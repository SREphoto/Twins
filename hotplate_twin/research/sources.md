# Research Sources — Laboratory Hotplate Stirrer

Reference materials and specifications for the `hotplate_twin` digital twin package.

## Reference Instruments

1. **IKA RCT basic / RET control-visc**:
   - Industry gold-standard magnetic hotplate stirrer with white ceramic-coated aluminum heating plate.
   - Speed range: 50 to 1500 RPM.
   - Temperature range: Ambient to 310 °C.
   - Safety circuit: Adjustable 50 to 360 °C.
   - PT1000 immersion temperature probe compatibility with automatic probe detection.
   - Residual heat indicator active above 50 °C.

2. **Corning PC-420D / PC-620D**:
   - Digital hotplate stirrer with Pyroceram ceramic top plate.
   - Microprocessor-controlled closed loop heating and stirring.

## Engineering Standards

- **DIN 12878**: Electrical laboratory equipment — Laboratory hotplates and heating blocks.
- **IEC 61010-1 / IEC 61010-2-010**: Safety requirements for electrical equipment for measurement, control, and laboratory use — Particular requirements for laboratory equipment for the heating of materials.
- **DIN 912 / ISO 4762**: Hexagon socket head cap screws.
- **IEC 60320-1 C14**: Appliance coupler for laboratory instrumentation.
- **DIN 41524**: 5-pin DIN connector for external PT1000 temperature sensor interface.
