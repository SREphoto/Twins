# Rotary Evaporator (Rotovap)

## Operation Principle

A rotary evaporator concentrates or purifies liquid samples by removing solvents through evaporation under reduced pressure. The sample is placed in a round-bottom flask that rotates partially submerged in a heated water bath. Rotation creates a thin film of liquid on the inner flask wall, maximizing surface area for evaporation. The vacuum pump reduces system pressure, lowering the solvent's boiling point so evaporation occurs at lower temperatures — protecting heat-sensitive compounds. Vapor rises into the condenser (typically a glass coil or "cold finger"), where refrigerant or chilled water circulates to cool it back to liquid. Condensed solvent drips into a separate collection flask (receiving flask). The system operates on three variables: bath temperature, rotation speed, and vacuum level — balancing these controls the evaporation rate.

## Key Functions

1. **Solvent removal** — concentrate non-volatile samples by evaporating volatile solvents
2. **Sample drying** — remove residual solvents from purified compounds
3. **Solvent recovery** — collect and recycle expensive or hazardous solvents
4. **Fraction collection** — sequential collection of different boiling point fractions
5. **Gentle concentration** — evaporate without thermal degradation using reduced pressure
6. **Crystallization preparation** — concentrate solutions until crystals form

## Complete Control Panel

### Buchi R-300 (Interface Panel / "Rotavapor R-300")

- **Main Power Switch** — rocker switch, I/O symbols, turns entire unit on/off
- **Touchscreen (4.3" color TFT)** — primary interface for all parameters:
  - **Heating ON/OFF** — virtual button to enable/disable bath heating
  - **Rotation Speed** — set value display + up/down arrows, range 20–280 rpm
  - **Bath Temperature Setpoint** — numeric input + confirm, range 20–220°C
  - **Bath Actual Temperature** — live readout from PT1000 sensor
  - **Operation Mode Selector**: Manual / Automatic / Program
  - **Lift Control** — virtual up/down buttons for motorized lift (V-300 vacuum controller)
  - **Vacuum Setpoint** (if V-300 connected) — mbar/hPa display + set value
  - **Timer** — countdown timer with auto-lift stop function
  - **Program Editor** — up to 10 stored programs, each with steps
  - **Settings** — language, units (°C or °F), calibration, brightness
- **Emergency Stop** — red push-button (optional), cuts all power
- **USB-A Port** — on side for data export / firmware update
- **RS-232 Port** — for PC connection / software (Buchi Data Recorder)

### Physical Controls on Water Bath (B-301 Heating Bath)

- **Temperature Set Knob** — rotary encoder with push-to-confirm, 20–220°C
- **Heating Indicator LED** — amber LED, illuminated when heating element active
- **Over-Temperature Cutout** — physical safety thermostat, manual reset button (red, recessed)
- **Drain Valve** — plastic turn-knob at rear-bottom of bath

### Controls on Condenser / Frame

- **Manual Lift Lever** — mechanical lever on right side of frame (non-motorized versions) for raising/lowering flask into bath
- **Rotation Start/Stop Switch** — toggle or push-button on motor housing
- **Rotation Speed Dial** — rotary potentiometer knob, 20–280 rpm (non-touchscreen models)
- **Vacuum Release Valve** — glass or PTFE stopcock on condenser head, turn to vent system to atmosphere
- **Solvent Recovery Tap** — PTFE stopcock at bottom of receiving flask drain

## Physical Features on the 3D Model

### Glassware Assembly

- **Evaporating Flask (Round-Bottom Flask)** — 50 mL to 5 L, attached via NS 29/32 or 24/40 ground glass joint, secured with plastic clip
- **Vapor Tube** — angled glass tube carrying vapor from flask to condenser
- **Condenser** — vertical or diagonal glass tube, typically:
  - **Cooling Water Inlet** (hose barb, lower port) — connects to water supply via silicone tubing
  - **Cooling Water Outlet** (hose barb, upper port) — returns to drain
  - **Cold Finger / Coil** — internal glass coil or double-jacket for coolant flow
- **Receiving Flask** — round or pear-shaped 100 mL–2 L, collects condensed solvent
- **Vacuum Connection Port** — glass or PTFE port on top of condenser, 8–10 mm hose barb for vacuum tubing
- **Vent Valve (Stopcock)** — PTFE or glass key, between system and vacuum port, turn 90° to vent
- **Drip Stopper / Splash Guard** — PTFE or glass insert in vapor tube to prevent carryover

### Frame and Drive

- **Motor Housing** — top-mounted, contains DC motor, rotation sensor, lift mechanism
- **Rotation Clutch** — mechanical coupling between motor and vapor tube
- **Spring-Loaded Bearing** — allows glass assembly weight to be supported
- **Lift Column** — vertical stainless steel or aluminum rod, motorized or manual
- **Lift Mechanism** — linear actuator inside column (motorized) or rack-and-pinion (manual)
- **Base Plate** — cast metal with rubber feet, contains power supply / controller electronics
- **Retort Stand Rod** — horizontal bar for clamping condenser

### Heating Bath (B-301 / HB 10)

- **Bath Basin** — stainless steel, ~2–4 L capacity
- **Heating Element** — sheathed resistive heater, 1000–1300 W
- **Temperature Sensor** — PT1000 RTD probe, immersed in bath fluid
- **Bath Cover** — plastic or silicone ring with segments, reduces heat loss and splashing
- **Drain Port** — at lowest point of basin, with plug or valve
- **Bath Feet** — 4 rubber feet, adjustable height for alignment

### Condenser Cooling Connections (Rear)

- **Cooling Water Inlet** — 6–8 mm ID hose barb, blue or green colored
- **Cooling Water Outlet** — 6–8 mm ID hose barb, red or uncolored
- **Recirculating Chiller Connection** — optional, replaces tap water with glycol loop

### Vacuum Pump Interface

- **Vacuum Tubing Port** — 8 mm hose barb on condenser top, connects to vacuum pump
- **Pressure Sensor Port** — optional, on condenser head for vacuum controller
- **Vacuum Controller Connection** — cable port on rear of controller unit (if integrated)
