    FTIR-7000x Optical, DSP & Electrical Schematics

    Michelson Interferometer & Single-Bounce Diamond ATR Evanescent Wave

    Mid-IR radiation from the 1200°C Polaris Ever-Glo Ceramic Source is collimated onto a 45° Ge-coated KBr Beamsplitter. One half reflects to the Fixed Gold Reference Mirror while the transmitted half reflects off the Voice-Coil Moving Mirror (tracked to $\pm 0.5\text{ nm}$ by the 632.8 nm HeNe Reference Laser). The recombined modulated beam bounces off the under-deck 45° ZnSe Input Mirror into the Type IIa Diamond ATR Crystal, generating an evanescent wave in the sample before reaching the DTGS Pyroelectric Detector.
    Interferogram: I(δ) = ∫ B(ν̃) · cos(2π · ν̃ · δ) dν̃  |  Fast Fourier Transform → %T(ν̃) = [I_sample(ν̃) / I_bg(ν̃)] × 100%

    Front-Right Bay DSP Motherboard (Assembly_Motherboard_DSP)

    Seated directly beneath the sloped touchscreen console, the 6-layer FR-4 motherboard integrates a TI TMS320C6748 32-Bit Floating-Point DSP (with anodized heatsink), Analog Devices AD7799 24-Bit Sigma-Delta ADC (connected via shielded RG-174 50Ω gold SMA coax to the DTGS detector), 8 GB eMMC Flash, Dual DDR3 SDRAM, HeNe HV Ballast Inverter, and 3 Modular Daughterboards (Voice-Coil Servo, IR Source Regulator, Load-Cell/Purge Telemetry).

    Hard Circuit Continuity & Mid-Right Bay SMPS

    Electrical continuity requires both the Benchtop NEMA 5-15P Plug (`isPluggedIn`) and the Rear Illuminated Rocker Switch (`switchOn`) to be closed (`hasPower = isPluggedIn && switchOn`). The open-frame SMPS (`Subsystem_PowerSupply_SMPS`) with toroidal transformer, dual 450V filter caps, 50mm side fan + exhaust duct, and 6-position barrier strip delivers regulated `+24V`, `+12V`, and `+5V` rails to the DSP motherboard.
