SREdesigns Unveils Groundbreaking Suite of 1:1 Scientific Digital Twins with Genuine Internal Anatomy and Dual-Truth Metrology

"Most 3D models on the web are hollow shells with painted-on textures that misrepresent how laboratory instruments actually function. In our twins, every single screw has real depth. If you pull the power cord from the wall outlet, the machine instantly loses power: the LCD blacks out, the drive motor coasts to zero, and the heaters shut off."

AUSTIN, Texas & CAMBRIDGE, Mass. — SRE Laboratory Systems (SREdesigns) today announced the open-access release of its next-generation Digital Twin Suite, bringing unprecedented mechanical fidelity, physical circuit continuity, and internal working electromechanical anatomy to web-based simulation.

The suite encompasses 18 scientific instruments—spanning tablet disintegration (USP ⟨701⟩), dissolution (USP ⟨711⟩), 4-decimal analytical microbalances, 15,000 RPM centrifuges, research spectrometers, and electron microscopy—alongside the expansive 14-zone Grand Research Facility.
