* **Spoken Voiceover**:
> "The Erweka Tablet Disintegration Tester is a benchtop laboratory instrument used by pharmaceutical scientists and quality control analysts to measure how long it takes for solid medicine tablets and capsules to completely break apart in liquid. When a patient swallows a pill, the pill must first soften, break down, and pass into solution before the active medicine can be absorbed by the body. This machine recreates the physical environment of the human stomach. It consists of a clear heated water bath held steadily at thirty-seven degrees Celsius, two one-liter glass beakers filled with test fluid, and two stainless steel basket assemblies. Mechanically, an internal electric motor turns a gearbox and crank arm that moves the baskets up and down thirty times every minute across a fifty-five millimeter stroke. Two independent motorized lead screws automatically lower the baskets into the fluid when a test begins and raise them out of the beakers when the timer finishes. As the tablets move through the warm fluid, the liquid softens the pill binder, and the movement washes the particles through the mesh screen. The operator then verifies that no firm core remains, and the digital controller records the exact disintegration time for compliance records."
