High-Pressure Stirred Reactor

The Parr 4560 Mini Reactor (450 mL capacity) is designed for chemical reactions under elevated pressures and temperatures, reaching up to 200 bar (3000 psi) and 350°C.

Key Features:

  • Magnetic Drive: Uses static coupling to transmit torque from the blue overhead motor through a steel wall, eliminating dynamic shaft seals and preventing high-pressure leaks.
  • Rushton Turbine: A 6-blade impeller mixes the reaction media at speeds up to 1200 RPM, promoting gas dispersion and thermal uniformity.
  • Thermowell & Dip Tube: Thermocouple probe monitors interior temperature, while the dip tube permits liquid sampling under pressure.

Operational Protocol:

Ensure the 6 heavy closure bolts are torqued cross-pattern to seat the flat PTFE gasket before gas charging. Check burst disc integrity before run.

Thermodynamics & Heat Transfer

Reactors require precise thermal management to avoid runaway exothermic reactions or reaction stall.

Heat input & Dissipation:

A hinged Clamshell Heater wraps around the vessel. Heat transfers through the thick stainless steel walls into the liquid. Active stirring minimizes thermal gradients.

Safety Relief:

  • Burst Disc: A thin metallic diaphragm calibrated to rupture at 220 bar, venting the reactor to prevent catastrophic vessel failure.
  • Safety Interlock: Omega PID controller shuts down heater power if the internal thermocouple detects temperatures exceeding 350°C.

NGSS Standards Alignment

This virtual lab showcase aligns with key High School Science standards:

HS-PS1-5 (Rates of Chemical Reactions):

Allows investigation of how increasing reactant concentration (under pressure) and kinetic energy (temperature) speeds up reaction rates.

HS-PS3-3 (Thermal Energy Transfer):

Students model, build, and test devices that transfer thermal energy, evaluating thermal conductivity, insulation, and system design limitations.

HS-ETS1-3 (Analyzing Solutions & Safety):

Evaluates complex safety mechanisms (alarms, burst discs, automatic interlocks) that mitigate risk in engineering designs.
